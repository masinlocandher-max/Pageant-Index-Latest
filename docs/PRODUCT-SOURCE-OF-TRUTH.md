# PAGEANT INDEX — PRODUCT SOURCE OF TRUTH

Status: Canonical
Version: 1.0

## 1. Product definition

**Pageant Index is the global index and operating infrastructure for pageantry.**

It maintains structured, durable records of legitimate people, organizations, pageants, editions, roles, results and professional relationships. Its core products are **Index, Vote, Tabulate and Credential**, supported by **Privileges** for Indexed members and a selective **Elite** layer.

Pageant Index is not an open social network and not a pay-to-list directory. Public users may search and use Pageant Index without becoming Indexed.

## 2. Core product pillars

### INDEX
The authoritative structured registry of pageantry.

### VOTE
Official organization-authorized public/fan voting infrastructure supporting free, paid or hybrid voting models.

### TABULATE
Competition scoring, judging, computation, audit, result locking and controlled publication infrastructure.

### CREDENTIAL
Document-based self-indexing, identity/credential review and Pageant Index NFC Tap ID credentials.

### PRIVILEGES
Benefits negotiated with participating partners for eligible Indexed individuals. Elite members may receive enhanced privileges.

## 3. Who can be Indexed

Eligible identities include legitimate pageant participants and professionals such as candidates, titleholders, owners, directors, judges, coaches, camps, designers, stylists, hair and makeup artists, photographers, videographers, choreographers, creative directors, show producers, hosts, production teams, crown/sash/trophy suppliers, technical suppliers, venues, hotels, PR/media/branding professionals, pageant technology providers, wellness partners, pageant schools, tourism offices and other parties with a documentable pageantry relationship.

Eligibility is based on a legitimate, supportable relationship to pageantry, not payment alone.

## 4. Ways to enter the Index

### A. Pageant Index invitation
Pageant Index may invite a legitimate industry participant or organization.

### B. Self-indexing application
An eligible individual or organization may apply directly by submitting documents supporting identity, role, credential or pageant history.

Self-indexing follows:

**Application → document review → eligibility approval → indexing/credential fee → published profile + Tap ID Card**

Payment does not guarantee approval, verification or confirmation of claims.

The approved indexing and credential fee includes, subject to the selected package:

- eligibility/document review;
- creation/publication of the official Pageant Index profile;
- permanent Pageant Index ID;
- initial credential record;
- physical NFC Tap ID Card;
- QR access to the canonical profile;
- digital credential/profile access;
- card production, with shipping/delivery governed by the applicable terms.

The full indexing/card fee should normally be collected after basic eligibility approval to avoid making inclusion appear purchasable.

### C. Organization submission
A recognized organization may submit people and relationships connected to its official pageant operations.

### D. Automatic event eligibility through Pageant Index services
When an organizer contracts Pageant Index for **Voting, Tabulation, or both**, legitimate people and organizations formally working in that edition become automatically **indexable** for their documented event roles.

They do not need to repeat the normal self-indexing eligibility application for that event relationship.

## 5. Automatic indexing through Voting and Tabulation

An organization using Pageant Index Voting and/or Tabulation may supply its official event roster. Eligible participants may include:

- official candidates/delegates;
- titleholders, winners and award recipients;
- pageant owner/director/franchise leadership;
- judges and competition officials;
- hosts/presenters where formally part of the event;
- creative and production teams;
- designers, coaches, stylists, photographers, videographers, makeup/hair professionals;
- formally credited suppliers and partners;
- other legitimate workers whose roles are documented by the organizer.

The organizer's roster is evidence of the specific event relationship. Pageant Index may create an **unclaimed Indexed profile** so the historical record exists even before the person creates an account.

Example state:

**Indexed · Organization Confirmed · Profile Unclaimed**

Automatic event eligibility does **not** give the organizer authority to verify unrelated claims. Only the documented role in the contracted edition receives organization-confirmed status.

People indexed through the official event record do not need to pay the normal self-indexing fee merely to preserve that specific event participation. A Tap ID Card is included only when the organizer's service package explicitly covers cards, or when the individual separately orders/qualifies for one.

## 6. Trust states

These states must never be collapsed into one generic checkmark.

- **Indexable** — meets a valid pathway for potential inclusion.
- **Indexed** — Pageant Index has created/published the record.
- **Identity Verified** — identity has undergone separate verification.
- **Credential Verified** — a specific credential has been independently supported.
- **Candidate Confirmed** — a candidate confirms a candidate-specific professional relationship.
- **Organization Confirmed** — an organization confirms an official edition-level role.
- **Unclaimed** — a legitimate public record exists but no user has yet claimed control of it.

Money cannot purchase verification, false credentials, titles, confirmation states, results or fabricated relationships.

## 7. Relationship graph

The core graph is:

**Pageant + Edition/Year + Person/Organization + Role + Relationship + Evidence/Confirmation**

Candidate-specific supplier relationships and official organization-level relationships are distinct.

Example:

- Photographer for Candidate X · Miss Example 2027 · Candidate Confirmed
- Official Photographer · Miss Example 2027 · Organization Confirmed

The moat is the durable relationship record: who worked with whom, in what pageant, in what year, in what role, and who confirmed it.

## 8. Permanent edition records

Each pageant edition can become a permanent canonical record containing:

- organization;
- edition/year;
- location/date;
- candidates;
- titleholders and placements;
- judges;
- organization team;
- official suppliers;
- candidate-specific professionals;
- voting campaign;
- tabulation configuration;
- official results;
- special awards;
- professional credits;
- confirmation/evidence states.

The record persists after the event as institutional history.

## 9. Voting product

Voting is a primary Pageant Index product and must belong to a real organization-authorized edition.

Supported models may include:

- free voting;
- paid voting;
- hybrid free + paid voting;
- vote limits;
- configured voting windows;
- candidate-specific voting pages;
- real-time or delayed public totals;
- organizer controls;
- abuse/fraud controls;
- transaction/audit records;
- official voting-result publication.

Fan/public voting is separate from judging unless an organizer explicitly defines it as part of the official scoring formula.

## 10. Tabulation product

Tabulation is a primary Pageant Index product. It should support:

- authenticated/scoped judge invitations;
- competition rounds;
- criteria and weighting;
- candidate scoring;
- score validation;
- missing-score detection;
- configurable tie-breaking;
- optional high/low score rules;
- authoritative system computation;
- round locking;
- audit trail;
- result certification;
- controlled publication;
- permanent result preservation.

The client/browser is never treated as the authoritative final calculator in production. The frontend prototype may simulate calculations locally until backend services are connected.

When Pageant Index officially tabulates an edition, the permanent edition record may display **Official Result · Tabulated by Pageant Index** after appropriate certification.

## 11. Tap ID Card

The physical Pageant Index credential is an NFC-enabled Tap ID Card linked to the holder's live canonical Pageant Index profile.

The credential may contain:

- public/professional name;
- Pageant Index ID;
- category/role;
- NFC tap;
- QR fallback;
- current Indexed/Verified/Elite status as applicable.

A tap or scan opens the live profile rather than relying on static printed claims.

## 12. Indexed Privileges

Eligible Indexed individuals may access participating **Pageant Index Privileges**. These are partner benefits, not guaranteed universal discounts.

Possible categories include:

- hotels/resorts and event accommodation;
- restaurants/cafés;
- beauty, salon, wellness, dental, fitness and aesthetic partners;
- photographers, designers, stylists, coaches, makeup artists and other pageant services;
- gowns, crowns, sashes, trophies and event suppliers;
- stage, lighting, sound, LED and production services;
- transportation, transfers, rentals and travel partners;
- studios, coworking, printing and business services;
- relevant software/technology;
- workshops, masterclasses and training;
- selected Pageant Index services such as replacement/additional Tap Cards;
- partner events, registrations and ticket offers.

Public wording should be defensible, for example:

**Show, scan or tap your active Pageant Index credential to access offers from participating partners. Privileges vary by partner, location and availability.**

The partner verification view should show only what is necessary, such as active status, name, Pageant Index ID and eligible privilege tier.

### Privilege tiers

- **Indexed** — useful partner privileges available to active eligible Indexed members.
- **Verified** — may unlock selected trust-based benefits where a partner requires stronger identity assurance.
- **Elite** — enhanced/premium benefits such as stronger offers, priority access, exclusive packages or VIP experiences when commercially secured.

Do not promise fixed global discount percentages unless contractually guaranteed by the specific partner.

## 13. Elite

**Pageant Index Elite** is a selective recognition/privilege layer for eligible established pageant figures, not a generic paid Pro account.

Possible eligible members include recognized titleholders, significant former titleholders, pageant owners/directors and other established pageant figures.

Payment may cover an Elite privilege package or credential after eligibility, but cannot manufacture standing.

## 14. Revenue model

Priority revenue sources:

1. Voting service/platform fees and eligible transaction participation.
2. Tabulation service packages.
3. Combined Voting + Tabulation organizer packages.
4. Self-indexing/credential fees after eligibility approval.
5. Organizer Tap ID Card packages for candidates, judges, teams or accredited professionals.
6. Elite membership/credential packages after eligibility.
7. Partner commissions/referrals where appropriate.
8. Sponsorship and clearly labelled commercial activations.
9. Optional processing/review fees where transparent and legally/commercially appropriate.

Commercial products must remain structurally separate from trust decisions.

## 15. What is never for sale

Pageant Index must never sell:

- fabricated eligibility;
- identity or credential verification without evidence;
- candidate/organization confirmations that did not occur;
- titles or placements;
- official results;
- fake relationships;
- undisclosed ranking manipulation;
- fabricated prestige;
- removal or alteration of legitimate factual records merely because someone pays.

## 16. Global strategy

Pageant Index is global from day one, with the Philippines as the first concentrated operating and data market. Country expansion occurs inside the same global Index rather than through disconnected country products.

## 17. SEO and AI discoverability

Public records should use stable canonical URLs, semantic HTML, structured data, descriptive metadata, durable identifiers, explicit relationship links and crawlable public pages.

Pageant Index should make records machine-readable but must not promise Google rankings, AI recommendations or third-party citation outcomes.

## 18. Frontend-first implementation rule

The current build phase is deliberately **frontend only**.

Do not add a backend, production auth, payment gateway, database dependency, email service or paid infrastructure yet.

The goal is to code the full operational product experience first, especially the **Admin Console**, using local/browser state and realistic seeded data so workflows can be tested end to end.

Backend services will be connected only after the product flows, data model, trust rules and admin operations are stable.

## 19. Admin-first operating model

Most Pageant Index work happens through the Admin Console. It is therefore a first-class product, not an afterthought.

The admin must be able to operate:

- dashboard/operating overview;
- indexing applications;
- Indexed profiles;
- unclaimed profiles;
- document/eligibility review;
- verification/confirmation states;
- organizations;
- pageants and editions;
- event rosters and automatic indexing;
- voting campaigns;
- tabulation rounds, judges, criteria, scores and results;
- Tap ID Card issuance/status;
- partners and Indexed/Verified/Elite privileges;
- Elite eligibility/status;
- trust/dispute flags;
- audit-style activity history;
- product/system settings.

During frontend-only development, admin mutations should persist in local browser storage and be clearly marked as prototype/local state.
